Hello! Welcome to my net home.

Hi, I'm Roel Cezar, but I go by Cabz. I'm a software engineer & developer based in the Philippines . I have a real passion for user interface and visual design, and I love creating experiences that feel intuitive, dynamic, and easy to use.

Give me enough coffee and I'll happily follow an idea wherever it leads. I get inspired easily, and I'm never satisfied with just thinking of something new; I feel an almost impulsive need to build it and see it come to life.

So far, that drive has turned into responsive website designs, front-end assets, and custom WordPress builds, and I'm always looking for the next thing to make.
