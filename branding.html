OBJECTIVE
Build a personal brand identity from the ground up, one that reflects my dual nature as a software engineer and visual designer. The system needed to feel modern, intentional, and adaptable across digital and print applications.

TOOLS USED
[Ai] Adobe Illustrator
[Ps] Adobe Photoshop

SCOPE
Logo design & construction, typography selection, color palette, brand guidelines, and visual language for digital applications._

Logo Construction

The mark is built from geometric fundamentals: every curve, angle, and proportion is derived from a structured grid system. Nothing is arbitrary.

Dark background
Light background
01Geometric Precision
Every curve follows a calculated radius. The letterforms sit on a baseline grid, maintaining optical balance across all sizes.
02Scalability
Designed to hold clarity from favicon size to billboard scale. No detail is lost at any resolution.
03Dual Identity
The mark carries both technical precision and creative warmth, reflecting the intersection of engineering and design.
